Hi, I'm Jinghe Cai

I graduated from Xiamen University with a BFA in Fine Arts.
Twelve of my paintings lined the hallway for my graduation show.
They were all sold on the first day.

My first comic book was published.
A friend went to a bookstore just to find it.
She texted me, “I’m proud of you.”

Dove into affective computing. Days and nights blurred in the lab.
I attended conferences and published my first first-author paper.
It felt like a step closer to a PhD.

Multiple paper rejections made me reflect:
whether research ever reached the real world.

I wavered between staying in academia
and moving toward industry.

I brought my work to CHI 2023 and quietly stepped away
from academia. In the years after, I stayed on as a reviewer
for ACM CHI and MM. A soft goodbye.

That year, I graduated from
Tsinghua University with a
MCS in Computer Science.

I took a half-year gap to publish my second book.
Then I came to the U.S., ready to explore design in the real world.
Now I’m a UX Design grad student at SCAD!

Years of HCI research shaped how I think and design, helping me stand out.
I led client projects with Microsoft and Samsung, turning design into impact.
One apple at a time, the story continues...
