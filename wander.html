Wander

Gamified Sleep Experience
Oct 2021, Research Work

Nighttime phone use is a major cause of sleep disorders. We introduce lightweight interventions across the sleep cycle to gently modify this behavior. At its core is a breath-controlled audio game that reframes the smartphone as a sleep aid. By gamifying breathing exercises, it turns the source of the problem into the solution for healthier sleep.
