During my work with Lepal.ai, I led a narrative-driven redesign of the UX and brand for a Gen Z mental wellness app, addressing early drop-off through a clearer product structure, narrative, and experience.

The redesign launched in Nov 2024, increasing Day-7 retention from 31% to 87% and growing weekly active users past 1,000.

My deliverables

  1. Defined a new brand tone from user research, anchoring the redesign.
  2. Restructured the Information Architecture from a flat 2D layout into a 3D spatial model.
  3. Elevated the visual identity system, redefining color, motion, and typography for a Gen Z-native experience.
  4. Owned end-to-end design for 5 core features, validated through A/B testing and cross-functional collaboration.

Background.

Lepal.ai is built on Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), the idea that changing how we think can change how we feel and behave. Traditional CBT relies on talk therapy and daily exercises, both of which require consistency to work.

Lepal.ai turns this process into lightweight, gamified, bite-sized interactions, making it easier for Gen Z to stay consistent. But users weren’t sticking around.

Challenge.

How might we help new users quickly feel LePal’s value and keep coming back?

The original Day 3 Magic Moment focused on solving users’ emotional problems, an outcome that takes time. I reframed it around a more immediate signal of value: making users feel heard and cared for.

Strategy 01Building a clearer information architecture

The existing structure made it difficult for users to understand what each feature did, how they connected, and where to start.

Design principles.

With early success redefined, I translated it into three design principles: make the experience clear, meaningful, and rewarding to return to.
