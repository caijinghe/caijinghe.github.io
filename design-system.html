Dock Experiment

Shelved experiment, kept in experiments/navigation-bar/; the live site uses the original top header. Idea: Text tabs for the two kinds of work (Design is home), icons for utilities (labeled on hover). Over a plain page it is solid --chip gray; over imagery it turns to frosted --glass, so the work shows through instead of being covered. Defined once in site-dock.js; a page only adds <nav class="site-dock" data-active="design|research|info">.

  • Current page: --ink fill, --paper text. Others: --muted text.
  • Tabs and icon buttons are 36px tall (tabs at least 96px wide), text 13px; groups pad 4px, 8px apart.
  • Surface switches automatically: site-dock.js checks what is under the dock (image, video, canvas, or a non-neutral background) and adds .is-over.
  • Info on the home page opens the experience popover above the dock.
  • Styles live in site-dock.css; tab text is --type-nav.

Work card & tags Canonical

Cards sit on --surface with a 12px radius and shrink to 0.98 on hover. Tags are white, 13px, and fade in at the bottom-left on hover.
